# Elementar 3.51.1-beta.1

First public release (beta). Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader 0.16+ and Fabric API.
Optional integrations: EMI (recipes), Jade (block info), Mod Menu (settings).

Highlights:
- All 118 elements and hundreds of real substances, each with a real composition.
- Eight historical tech levels (Stone Age to Accelerator age), unlocked by research.
- 70+ machines that hold each process's real temperature, pressure and catalyst, with power, pipes, tanks and belts.
- Multiblock structures (blast furnace, coke battery, tanks and more).
- Geological world type: real rocks, ores by rock type, rivers, karst, volcanoes, salt and fresh water.
- 138 tree species, 62 shrubs, 20 crops with hidden Mendelian genetics, and fauna.
- Field Guide, Field Atlas and compendiums for trees, plants, minerals and animals.
- Creative test items (infinite generator, fluid source, accelerator clock, and more).
- Ten languages: English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Danish and Russian.
