# Elementar 3.68.4-beta.1

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader 0.16+ and Fabric API. Optional: EMI, Jade, Mod Menu.
Beta: back up your worlds before updating.

Fixes:
- Fixed a freeze of the world's server shortly after joining: blocks broke without dropping anything, crafting stopped responding and "Saving world" never finished. Tree felling no longer looks at the world from world-generation threads.
- In the Geological world, rough stones (granite, diorite, andesite, tuff, basalt, smooth basalt, calcite and the mod's cobbled rocks) now craft into cobblestone, so a start in a granite craton or a volcanic plain is no longer stuck without cobblestone.
- If the single-player server ever stops ticking for 15 seconds, a thread dump is written to latest.log to help report it.
- Bone meal outside a plant's biome now reaches the server (smoke and a note).

New since 3.51.1:
- Space age (levels 8 to 10): rocket multiblock with real propellants and an optional reusable first stage, satellite types, meteorites and a Space Telescope, a tokamak fusion reactor.
- New dimensions: the Moon and Mars (suits, capsule, pressurised base, local resources), then Mercury (a moving dawn line) and a floating base above Venus's clouds. Fiction materials are clearly marked.
- Trees: loose leaves fall at once, and a cut trunk brings the tree down. Shrubs can be transplanted with a shovel, and give cuttings.
- Warnings when you look at a sapling or shrub planted outside its biome or in the wrong greenhouse.
- An Elementar advancement tab, and a starter Field Guide chapter "First steps".
- Fauna babies have no horns, antlers or tusks; texture and translation fixes.

# Elementar 3.51.1-beta.1

First public release (beta). Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader 0.16+ and Fabric API.
Optional integrations: EMI (recipes), Jade (block info), Mod Menu (settings).

Highlights:
- All 118 elements and hundreds of real substances, each with a real composition.
- Eight historical tech levels (Stone Age to Accelerator age), unlocked by research.
- 70+ machines that hold each process's real temperature, pressure and catalyst, with power, pipes, tanks and belts.
- Multiblock structures (blast furnace, coke battery, tanks and more).
- Geological world type: real rocks, ores by rock type, rivers, karst, volcanoes, salt and fresh water.
- 138 tree species, 62 shrubs, 20 crops with hidden Mendelian genetics, and fauna.
- Field Guide, Field Atlas and compendiums for trees, plants, minerals and animals.
- Creative test items (infinite generator, fluid source, accelerator clock, and more).
- Ten languages: English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Danish and Russian.
